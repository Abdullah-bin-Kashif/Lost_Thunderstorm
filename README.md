Submission for game dev 

Lost
"What we love is never really lost."

Lost is a compact, browser-based top-down action-adventure game built in a single self-contained HTML5 file. Play as Ahmed as he ventures through the twisted, supernatural rooms of his home to rescue his late grandmother Nani's framed picture from the jinn that stole it.📖 StoryNani passed away last winter, leaving behind a small framed picture that Ahmed sets beside him on the couch every evening. Tonight, the lights flickered, a cold hand rose from the cushions, and the couch swallowed the picture whole. With the house twisted by jinn, Ahmed must fight through five increasingly dangerous rooms to get her back.🎮 Gameplay & MechanicsExplore & Unlock: Navigate through grid-based rooms, shoot wooden crates to clear paths, and collect golden keys to unlock inner doors and exits.Twin-Stick Combat: Move and fire in four cardinal directions to defeat jinn lurking in the house.Timed Parries: Tap PARRY just as an attack connects to turn the tide:Chaser Jinn: Flash red right before lunging. Parrying their strike stuns them temporarily.Shooter Jinn: Fire purple energy bolts. Parrying reflects their shots back at double damage.Boss Jinn: Parrying its ring projectiles or red dash attacks stuns it, opening a window for heavy counter-attacks.Room Locks: Certain rooms seal their exits until all jinn (including ambushing waves) are defeated.🕹️ ControlsLost supports both touchscreens (recommended in landscape orientation) and desktop keyboards.ActionTouch ControlsKeyboard ControlsMoveDrag left thumb (virtual stick)W A S DShootDrag right thumb (virtual stick)↑ ↓ ← → (Arrow Keys)Parry / Advance TextTap the PARRY button / Tap screenSpacebar🏠 LevelsLiving Room – Learn movement and navigation; collect 1 key to open the exit.Kitchen and Hall – Shoot crates to open pathways and manage 3 keys across an inner door and the main exit.Bedroom – Face Chaser Jinn, master the parry timing, and clear the room to unlock the exit.Bathroom and Corridor – Deflect projectiles from stationary Shooter Jinn while navigating tight corridors.The Couch – Confront the multi-phase Boss Jinn, survive its bullet rings, dashes, and minions, and reclaim Nani's picture.🚀 Getting StartedBecause Lost has zero external dependencies, running it is instant:Download or clone Lost.html.Open Lost.html in any modern web browser (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge).(Optional) For mobile play, host the file locally or via GitHub Pages and open it in landscape mode for the best experience.🛠️ Technical HighlightsZero Dependencies: Written in pure vanilla HTML5, CSS3, and JavaScript (~12 KB total).Custom Canvas Engine: Features dynamic DPR scaling, screen-shake effects, invulnerability frames, virtual twin-stick input handling, and HTML5 Vibration API haptic feedback on supported mobile devices.ASCII Level Architecture: All 5 levels are parsed directly from compact character-grid strings (# walls, T furniture, C crates, K keys, J/S/B enemies).
